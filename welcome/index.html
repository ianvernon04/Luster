How it works

From booking to the treatment room, nothing slips through.

1They book online

Your own booking page, plus a Book button for your Wix, Squarespace or Instagram. Clients change or cancel from their own link, and a provider can never be double-booked.

2Their health history comes with them

New clients fill in their intake on their phone right after booking: conditions, medications, allergies, pregnancy, recent treatments. It takes about six minutes, and it's on your iPad before they walk in.

3You see the caution first

Accutane before a peel. Blood thinners before microneedling. Cancer treatment before a deep-tissue massage. Luster flags the clash on the appointment, so nobody finds out the hard way.

Everything the front desk does

Fast enough for a Saturday. Careful enough for a med spa.

A calendar that keeps up

Every provider side by side. Drag to reschedule, tap an empty slot to book. It keeps working when the Wi-Fi drops and catches up when it's back.

Consents for each service

The right consent for each treatment, signed on the client's phone or the iPad and filed with them. Nothing to print, nothing to lose.

One file per client

Visit history, treatment notes, caution flags, allergies, and before-and-after photos with a side-by-side slider.

Checkout and refunds

Services, retail, tips, discounts, refunds and store credit. Cards run on your own terminal and Luster records them.

Gift cards

Every card gets its own code, works on every iPad in the studio, and can't be spent twice.

Reports that matter

Revenue, rebooking rate, no-shows, and how full each provider's book is, by day, week or month.

Book from anywhere

A booking page with your menu, prices and team, a Book button for your website and Instagram, and a QR sign for the counter.

Private by design

Each studio's data is walled off. Health files open only for owners, managers and providers, and every look is logged.

iPad, phone or computer

Installs from the website, no app store. One studio, as many devices as you need, all in sync.

Questions

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

Founding studios lock in their price for as long as they stay. Request a demo and we'll walk you through it on the call.

Does it send text reminders?

Today Luster writes the confirmation or reminder, opens it in your phone's Messages ready to send, and logs it on the client's file. Automatic texts from your studio's number are on the way.

Can clients pay online or leave a deposit?

Not yet. You take cards on your own terminal and Luster records the payment. Online payments and deposits are on the roadmap.

How do I move my clients over?

Export your client list from your current software as a spreadsheet. We bring it in together: duplicates merged, notes and allergies kept.

Is my clients' health information safe?

Each studio's data is walled off from every other. Health files open only for owners, managers and providers, and every time someone opens one is logged. Luster doesn't sign HIPAA business associate agreements yet; if your practice needs one, tell us before you switch.

What do I need to run it?

An iPad, a phone or a computer with a web browser. Luster installs from this website, keeps working if the connection drops, and catches up when it's back.

Who's behind Luster?

Luster is made by New World Logic LLC, a Louisiana company. You deal with Ian, who builds it, not a ticket queue.
